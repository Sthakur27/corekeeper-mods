# Auto Loadout Switch

Auto Loadout Switch changes your equipment loadout when you select a linked item. You can link a pickaxe to your mining gear, a sword to your melee gear, or a bow to your ranged gear.

Hold an item, choose the loadout you want it to use, then press **Ctrl+L**. A message above your character confirms the link, and the item's tooltip shows which loadout it will select. You can rebind the key under **Controls > Auto Loadout Switch**.

Press the key again while holding that item in the same loadout to unlink it. To change its link, select a different loadout and press the key. The shortcut does not work while a menu or text input is open.

Links apply to an exact **item type**, so two copies of the same pickaxe share a link. Different item types can use different loadouts. Each character has separate links, saved on your PC in `AutoLoadouts/bindings.cfg`. Nothing is linked until you choose it.

The loadout changes only when you switch to a different linked item type. You can choose a loadout manually and keep it until you select another linked item. Unlinked items leave your loadout alone, and loading into a world does not change the loadout you logged out with. A link to a loadout that is no longer available is ignored.

Requires **CoreLib** and is intended for Core Keeper **1.3.0**. It works with **Five Loadouts** and **Vanity Slots & Shared Equipment**. This is a client-side mod: other players do not need to install it for you to use it.
