# More NPCs (Alpha)

More NPCs adds eight merchants you can bring home after boss fights. Each boss listed below drops an idol. Put it in an enclosed room with an unclaimed bed, and the matching merchant can move in.

Every kill drops another idol, so you can replace a lost one by fighting the boss again. You can have one of each merchant per world. If a merchant's bed is destroyed, it can move to another free bed in a room with its idol.

### Finding your merchants

Ottoline's decor shop contains **423 items in 29 consecutive theme groups**. Scroll the buy window with the mouse wheel to browse home furniture, lighting, potted plants, aquariums, architecture, biome furniture, ruins, seasonal sets, themed storage and collectible figurines. Kelp Trees, Alien Floor Vents, pillars, rugs, shelves, monuments and temple props are included. Theme grouping is the order of the stock, not separate tabs.

Common furnishings generally cost 25–400 Ancient Coins, rare display pieces 1,500, and uncommon/rare/epic figurines 1,000/2,500/5,000 before the price multiplier. Original stock retains its previous prices. Furniture restocks in lots of 10, rare display pieces 5, and figurines 3. The [full decor catalog](docs/decor-stock.csv) lists every item, theme and base price. As with other More NPCs goods, pricing uses the global item value, so sale values and other shops selling the same items are affected. This is a broad curated selection; machinery, boss summons, scene-only objects and separate cosmetic variations are not exhaustively included.

Nearby merchants appear on the map and minimap with their own idol icon; hover over a full-map icon to see the merchant's name. Markers follow the game's normal NPC synchronization range, so distant merchants disappear until you return nearby. This does not keep NPCs active across the whole world or add network messages.

Vanilla merchants also appear: Slime, Caveling, Fishing, Seasonal, Crystal and Void merchants use their respective summoning-item icons. Existing native markers are preserved.

Names appear above NPCs within eight tiles of your character, including vanilla merchants. Labels hide outside that range, with the full map, or in the game's hidden-UI mode. Each multiplayer client uses its own character's proximity; map/minimap markers are unaffected.

Host cleanup command: **`/morenpcs killall`** removes every More NPCs merchant currently loaded on the server, including earned merchants and duplicates. Vanilla NPCs, summoning idols and health values are unchanged. Existing valid idol rooms can summon replacements. Only the host/single-player user can run it; nothing runs automatically. The command reports the number queued for native removal.

Open **Settings > Mod Options > More NPCs** to change prices or stock:

- **Price multiplier** starts at 1x. Choices range from 0.25x to 4x and affect every buy price set by this mod.
- **Potions and explosives per restock** starts at 20 and can be set from 1 to 99. It also covers Old Hemlock's Recall Idol, vending snacks and drinks, and optional Potion of Return. Other items have fixed stock amounts.
- **NPC max health** sets the health of every merchant NPC, both this mod's and the game's own merchants. It starts at Default (100,000, the game's value). The other choices are 1,000, 10,000 and 1,000,000. Merchants already living in your world change within a few seconds and keep their share of health.

Sell prices stay at one fifth of the listed 1x buy price, regardless of the multiplier. The mod changes these items' prices everywhere they are traded, including vending machines. Vending items absent from these shops keep their vanilla prices; the Pest Egg is included in Ankhra's stock and does receive its new price.

The shop window displays 24 items at a time in an 8-by-3 grid. Old Hemlock and Tridra can have more than 24 wares: scroll with the mouse wheel over the shop to see the rest. Controllers can only reach the first 24 items, so Tridra's boss summons need a mouse.

## Requirements and alpha status

For Core Keeper 1.3.0. Install **CoreLib** and **Mod Options** as well as More NPCs on the host/server and every client.

This is an alpha. It has passed compilation, asset checks and loading in the game, but in-world gameplay and multiplayer have not yet been tested. Merchant arrival, trading and animations still need playtesting.

Back up your world before trying the alpha or removing it. This mod adds saved merchant and idol objects; loading and saving without it may remove those objects. Keep a backup made while the mod was installed if you want to restore them later.

The merchant and idol source art was generated with AI, then converted into pixel sprites. Animations use subtle breathing, sway and nods rather than individually drawn articulated poses.

Cover artwork generated with AI from the mod's own merchant and idol designs; not an in-game screenshot.
