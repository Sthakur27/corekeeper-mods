## Coordinator review

All eight boards were visually inspected. Grout's masonry, Ottoline's lampshade, Fizzwick's helmet, Ankhra's shell and Tilly's hat/pot have especially clear top planes. Tridra now reads as a creature with a substantial coiled body and three distinct heads. The set shares Hemlock's clustered material shading while retaining individual themes.

Before production: consider smaller, less cartoon-like eyes for Grout; simplify the ornament on Ankhra's 16px idol; make Wren's short bird beak explicit and improve the bell's visible top plane; check Tridra's side-facing heads and Hemlock's mask against the overhead camera. All generated boards still need crisp native-resolution pixel work. These are review observations, not approval to replace assets.
